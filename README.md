# TikTok Favorites Shuffler

## TikTok Favorites Shuffler is a web-based application that allows you to shuffle and enjoy your favorite TikTok videos in a random order. This tool provides a simple and intuitive interface to relive your favorite TikTok moments in a new and exciting way.

### Features
- Shuffle Favorites: Randomly rearrange your list of favorite TikTok videos.
- User-Friendly Interface: Easy-to-use design for a seamless experience.

### Usage
-Add Your Favorite Videos:

Input your favorite Videos.txt.

-Shuffle:

Click the "Shuffle" button to randomize the order of your favorite videos.

-Enjoy:

Browse through your shuffled list and enjoy your tiktok favorited videos.
